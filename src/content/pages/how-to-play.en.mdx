# How to Play Build a Hooper

![Retro tactical poster showing the five-stage Build a Hooper game loop](/imgs/generated/how-to-play-tactical-poster.webp)

Build a Hooper is an unofficial browser basketball simulator. You do not start with a finished player. You assemble one skill at a time from team-season rosters, then prove the sheet across an **82-game** season. This guide is the full loop — not a slogan.

If you only want the live game, open [/game](/game). If you want to understand _why_ a draft works before you burn rerolls, stay here.

## The full loop in five stages

Every run follows the same spine. Modes change how much information you see. They do not change the order.

1. **Choose a mode** — Classic, Blind, or Chaos ([modes guide](/modes)).
2. **Lock a position** — Classic lets you pick; Blind reveals it; Chaos hides it until the end.
3. **Draft 13 attributes** — each round: spin a team-season → pick one player → lock one open attribute from that player’s grades.
4. **Reveal the hooper** — overall rating and the full sheet appear together.
5. **Join a career team and simulate 82 games** — awards, seeding, playoffs, and (if you win) a title.

That is the entire product loop. Tips exist to help you survive stages 3–5 with a coherent identity instead of thirteen unrelated letter grades.

## What you are actually drafting

The sheet always uses the same thirteen skills:

**3PT · MID · FIN · DNK · HAN · PAS · PDEF · IDEF · BLK · REB · ATH · STR · CLU**

A full glossary lives on [/attributes](/attributes). For a first run, treat them as four jobs:

| Cluster          | Attributes           | Job on the floor                                                    |
| ---------------- | -------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Scoring          | 3PT, MID, FIN, DNK   | Put the ball in the basket from deep, mid, paint, and above the rim |
| Creation         | HAN, PAS             | Keep possessions alive and create for teammates                     |
| Defense / glass  | PDEF, IDEF, BLK, REB | Stop wings, protect paint, contest, rebound                         |
| Physical / close | ATH, STR, CLU        | Burst, contact strength, late-game execution                        |

Modern basketball analysis often drops rigid “five positions” language and talks in **roles** — floor general, shot creator, 3-and-D wing, stretch big, rim anchor ([Basketball Index offensive roles](https://www.bball-index.com/offensive-archetypes/), [theScore on hybrid roles](https://www.thescore.com/nba/news/1974726/forget-the-5-positions-inside-a-new-way-to-classify-nba-players)). Build a Hooper still asks you to draft at PG / SG / SF / PF / C, but the sheet rewards the same idea: **pick a role, then feed the attributes that role needs**.

## Round-by-round draft (the part that decides everything)

Each of the 13 rounds is one decision:

1. Spin a **team-season** (legendary and modern squads from the pool).
2. Read the roster grades for the attributes you still need.
3. Pick **one** player.
4. Lock **one** unused attribute from that player onto your sheet.
5. That player and that attribute are spent for this run.

You cannot stack five attributes from the same spin. You also cannot “save” a player for later once you leave the round. The game forces trade-offs: take the A+ handle now, or wait and hope another creator appears.

### Rerolls (do not waste them)

Reroll counts by mode:

- **Classic** — 3 team rerolls
- **Blind** — 1
- **Chaos** — 1

Reroll when the roster cannot support the role you named out loud after three locks. Do not reroll just because you missed a single A+. Seasons punish empty clusters more than a B+ in a secondary skill.

## First-run recipe (Classic only)

New players should stay in Classic until they can name a role without looking at a tip sheet.

1. Pick a position you understand — **PG** (creation) or **SF** (versatile wing) are forgiving teachers. See [/best-builds](/best-builds).
2. After three locks, say the role: “floor general,” “two-way wing,” “paint anchor.” If you cannot name it, you are collecting grades.
3. Spend early rounds on the **primary cluster** for that role. Cover one clear weakness before round 10.
4. Save at least one reroll for a dead spin that offers nothing in your identity.
5. Simulate the season and read game 40–60, not only the highlight box score. Consistency beats a loud opening week.

When Classic feels readable, move to Blind, then Chaos. Harder [achievement](/achievements) ladders lean on those darker drafts — that is intentional.

## After the reveal: season logic

Publishing the card is not the finish line. The **82-game** schedule grades average nights. Ask:

- Does this player still make sense in game 40 and game 60?
- Did I leave a zero in a job my position must do every night?
- Am I hoping for miracles from CLU while the rest of the sheet is hollow?

Sign in before you finish if you want the run saved for achievements and legacy tracking. Guests can learn the loop; progress sticks after login.
