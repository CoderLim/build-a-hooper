# Best Point Guard Build

The point guard’s job is still the oldest one in basketball: start the offense, protect the ball, and decide whether tonight is a pass-first or score-first game. Modern analysis splits that job into roles like **primary ball handler**, **shot creator**, and hybrid scorers ([Basketball Index](https://www.bball-index.com/offensive-archetypes/), [theScore hybrid roles](https://www.thescore.com/nba/news/1974726/forget-the-5-positions-inside-a-new-way-to-classify-nba-players)). In Build a Hooper, those roles map cleanly onto **HAN**, **PAS**, and a scoring axis (**3PT** or **MID**).

This is a guide, not a guaranteed overall. Play the draft on [/game](/game).

## Position identity

A usable PG sheet answers three questions by game 40:

1. Can I bring the ball up without dying? → **HAN**
2. Can I create an advantage for someone? → **PAS** (and secondary scoring)
3. When the defense takes away the first option, do I still have a two? → **3PT** or **MID**, with **FIN** as glue

Empty handles with pretty shooting is a shooting guard card wearing a PG label. Empty passing with elite scoring can work as a high-usage guard — name that route on purpose.

## Priority order (Classic)

Lock early (rounds 1–5), in roughly this order when the board allows:

1. **HAN**
2. **PAS**
3. **3PT** _or_ **MID** (pick one axis and commit)
4. **CLU** or **FIN**
5. **PDEF** / **ATH** as coverage

Leave **BLK / REB / IDEF** for leftovers unless you are forcing a novelty big-guard. Chaos is the exception — see below.

## Three build routes

### 1. Floor General

**Primary:** HAN · PAS  
**Secondary:** 3PT or MID for spacing threat · CLU  
**Skip early:** DNK maxing, pure BLK chasing

You run the offense. Scoring is real enough that help defenders cannot ignore you, but the card’s story is decision-making. In real basketball language this sits near pass-first / orchestrator profiles.

**Draft plan:** Secure HAN+PAS before round 7. Add one scoring skill by round 9. Use late locks for CLU and a touch of PDEF so you are not hunted every possession.

### 2. Shot Creator

**Primary:** HAN · 3PT or MID · FIN  
**Secondary:** PAS (keep it from collapsing) · ATH · CLU  
**Risk:** Starving PAS turns you into an iso island for 82 games

This is the high-usage guard who creates their own shot. You still want enough PAS to punish overhelps.

**Draft plan:** HAN first. Install the scoring axis next. Take PAS the moment a B+ or better appears — do not wait until round 12.

### 3. Two-Way Lead Guard

**Primary:** HAN · PDEF · PAS or 3PT  
**Secondary:** ATH · CLU · FIN  
**Identity check:** If PDEF is empty, you are not on this route

Pressure on the ball, then start offense. Harder to draft because defense and creation compete for early locks. Worth it when Classic shows an elite perimeter stopper who also handles.

## Draft phases

| Phase | Goal                                              |
| ----- | ------------------------------------------------- |
| 1–4   | HAN + start of PAS or scoring axis                |
| 5–9   | Finish the identity pair; add FIN or CLU          |
| 10–13 | PDEF/ATH glue; never leave both PAS and HAN empty |

## Mode notes

- **Classic:** Follow the routes above; three rerolls exist to escape creator-less boards.
- **Blind:** You know you are a PG. Draft as if grades are role tags — take known creators and lead guards even when letters are hidden.
- **Chaos:** Bias HAN + FIN + one defense. A pure PAS-only plan dies if you reveal as a wing or big.

## Common mistakes

| Mistake                       | Result                         | Fix                         |
| ----------------------------- | ------------------------------ | --------------------------- |
| 3PT before HAN                | Turnovers erase spacing        | Handles first               |
| Ignoring PAS on “scorer” PGs  | Iso death by February          | Keep secondary PAS          |
| Early CLU addiction           | Pretty closer, empty engine    | CLU after identity          |
| Classic greed into Chaos copy | Hidden position breaks snipers | Chaos = transferable skills |

## Illustrative donors (fan grades)
