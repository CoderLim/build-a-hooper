# Best Shooting Guard Build

Shooting guards exist to score — and, in the modern game, to space the floor without disappearing when the ball stops. Primers still list perimeter scoring, off-ball movement, and on-ball pressure as the SG toolkit ([position breakdowns](https://halloffame-online.com/blog/basketball-positions-explained-roles-responsibilities-skills/), [Nike positions](https://www.nike.com/a/positions-in-basketball)). Build a Hooper encodes that toolkit mainly as **3PT**, **MID**, **FIN**, plus enough **HAN** to punish closeouts.

## Position identity

A durable SG sheet needs:

1. A **primary scoring axis** (usually 3PT, sometimes MID-heavy)
2. A way to finish when you reject the three (**FIN**, sometimes **DNK**)
3. Just enough **HAN** to attack a hard closeout
4. Optional but powerful: **PDEF** so you are not a liability for 82 games

Off-ball shooter roles in public archetype work (movement / stationary / off-screen shooters) all still need a real shot diet — not only ATH cosmetics ([Basketball Index](https://www.bball-index.com/offensive-archetypes/)).

## Priority order (Classic)

1. **3PT** (default) or **MID** if the board refuses threes
2. **FIN** or second scoring skill
3. **HAN**
4. **ATH** / **CLU**
5. **PDEF** for two-way routes

## Three build routes

### 1. Movement Shooter

**Primary:** 3PT · ATH · HAN (enough to relocate and attack)  
**Secondary:** MID · CLU · PDEF crumbs  
**Avoid:** Spending early locks on BLK/REB

You are gravity. The card works when help has to fly at you. Keep a dab of handles so you are not glued to the catch.

### 2. Three-Level Scorer

**Primary:** 3PT · MID · FIN  
**Secondary:** HAN · CLU · ATH  
**Identity check:** If two of the three scoring levels are empty, rename the route

This is the SG who hurts you everywhere inside the arc and beyond. Harder to draft, filthy when Classic cooperates.

### 3. Two-Way Scorer

**Primary:** 3PT · PDEF · FIN or HAN  
**Secondary:** ATH · CLU  
**Trade-off:** You will sacrifice some pure offense for nights on the ball

Modern wings and bigger guards who can switch get paid in real basketball for a reason. In-game, PDEF early locks are how you join that club.

## Draft phases

| Phase | Goal                                           |
| ----- | ---------------------------------------------- |
| 1–4   | Install 3PT or MID; do not leave scoring empty |
| 5–9   | Add FIN/HAN; decide if you are two-way         |
| 10–13 | ATH/CLU/PDEF glue                              |

## Mode notes

- **Classic:** Hunt the scoring axis openly; reroll boards with no shooters if that is your entire identity.
- **Blind:** Prefer known scorers and 3-and-D wings by reputation when grades hide.
- **Chaos:** 3PT-only plans are fragile if you reveal as a big. Pair shooting with FIN or REB insurance.

## Common mistakes

| Mistake                                      | Fix                                       |
| -------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------- |
| DNK before 3PT/FIN                           | Dunks need a path to the rim              |
| Zero HAN on “shooter”                        | Closeouts will eat you                    |
| Ignoring PDEF then complaining about defense | Two-way is a route, not a vibe            |
| Copying PG PAS stacks                        | Secondary PAS is enough for most SG cards |

## Sample Classic lock order (Movement Shooter)

1. Best available **3PT**
2. Second scoring skill (**MID** or **FIN**) if 3PT already secure — else double down on 3PT
3. **HAN** the first time a B+ or better appears
4. **ATH**
5. **CLU**
   6–13. Fill PDEF, STR crumbs, never leave both FIN and MID empty if 3PT stalled early

If rounds 1–4 produce zero shooting, **reroll** in Classic. A Movement Shooter identity that starts with REB/BLK is already lost — rename the run or reset.

## Season checklist after reveal

- Games 1–20: Does help actually fly at your 3PT, or are you a non-shooter in disguise?
- Games 21–50: When the first option is taken away, do FIN/MID create a second bite?
- Games 51–82: Empty PDEF cards get hunted — did you leave enough defense to close series?

If overall looks high but the scoring axis is one-note, expect variance spikes, not stable seeds. Read [/attributes](/attributes) before blaming the simulator.

## When to abandon the route mid-draft

- **Movement Shooter → Three-Level:** Board keeps offering MID/FIN while refusing a second 3PT — lean in.
- **Any scorer → Two-Way:** Elite PDEF appears early and your 3PT is already A- — take the defense lock.
- **SG plan → SF tools:** You are stacking REB/IDEF with weak shooting — you may already be drafting a forward. Consider whether Chaos reveal risk applies.
