## Build a Hooper Modes

![Retro triptych contrasting clear, hidden, and chaotic basketball draft information](/imgs/generated/modes-tactical-poster.webp)

Modes do not rewrite the thirteen attributes. They change **information** and **reroll budget**. Same draft spine, different fog of war. If you are new, read [/how-to-play](/how-to-play) first, then come back here to choose how hard the puzzle should be.

## Quick comparison

| Mode        | Position             | Ratings while drafting    | Team rerolls | Best for                              |
| ----------- | -------------------- | ------------------------- | ------------ | ------------------------------------- |
| **Classic** | You choose           | Visible                   | 3            | Learning clusters and role identity   |
| **Blind**   | Revealed first       | Hidden until final reveal | 1            | Committing without grade shopping     |
| **Chaos**   | Hidden until the end | Hidden until the end      | 1            | Instinct drafts and hard achievements |

There is no separate “Daily” mode on buildahooper.org right now. Competitive pressure comes from Blind / Chaos, the [leaderboard](/leaderboard), and [achievements](/achievements) that count Chaos/Blind rings harder than Classic farming.

## Classic — full scouting

Classic is the cleanest classroom. You pick PG / SG / SF / PF / C, see letter grades on every roster player, and keep three rerolls.

**How to approach it**

- Name a role early (floor general, movement shooter, paint anchor). Modern scouting talk uses role language more than rigid position boxes — useful even inside a five-position draft ([Nike’s position primer](https://www.nike.com/a/positions-in-basketball), [Hall of Fame Online position skills](https://halloffame-online.com/blog/basketball-positions-explained-roles-responsibilities-skills/)).
- Use visible grades to fill a **primary cluster** first, then patch one weakness.
- Spend rerolls on empty boards, not on chasing every A+.

**When Classic lies to you**

Seeing every grade invites greed. Players max scoring and forget handles, or build a wall with no finishing. The season still plays 82 games. Classic teaches the sheet; it does not forgive a hollow identity.

## Blind — position known, grades dark

Blind reveals your position, then hides ratings until the final reveal. You get one reroll.

**How to approach it**

- Draft from **position job**, not from hunting letter grades you cannot see.
- Prefer players whose known roles match the spot — creators for PG, wings who shoot and defend for SF, glass eaters for C — even when the numbers are blacked out.
- Keep a second skill path. If the first five spins refuse creation on a PG run, pivot toward a scoring guard identity instead of panic-rerolling your only charge.

**Why Blind exists**

Real scouting also works with incomplete boards. You know the job (point guard) before you know tonight’s exact skill grades. Blind trains that habit. Several achievement ladders only count Chaos/Blind titles — Classic farming will not clear the whole board.

## Chaos — build in the dark

Chaos hides position and ratings until the end. One reroll. Builds that need perfect information fall apart here on purpose.

**How to approach it**

- Draft **balanced clusters**, not a hyper-specialized sniper that only works at one position.
- Prefer attributes that travel: finishing + athleticism + one creation skill + one defense skill will survive more position reveals than a pure catch-and-shoot sheet with empty paint tools.
- Accept variance. Chaos is for players who already survived Classic and Blind without melting down on a dead spin.

**Chaos and achievements**

If you care about Complete Hooper, underdog runs, or long Chaos/Blind ring ladders, Chaos is not optional flavor — it is the grind lane. See [/achievements](/achievements).

## Mode strategy by draft phase

### Rounds 1–4 — identity

- Classic: lock the two or three attributes that define the role.
- Blind / Chaos: lock transferable skills (HAN or FIN or REB depending on the board) and avoid painting yourself into a single shot diet.

### Rounds 5–9 — coverage

Cover the job your position must do every night. A PG without PAS/HAN, a C without REB/IDEF, an SG without a scoring axis — these fail long seasons even if overall looks fine.

### Rounds 10–13 — glue

Fill holes. CLU is valuable late; it is not a substitute for an empty primary cluster. ATH and STR matter more when the sheet already has a real offensive or defensive job.

## Common mistakes

| Mistake                                | Why it hurts                             | Fix                             |
| -------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------- | ------------------------------- |
| Jumping to Chaos on day one            | You learn panic, not roles               | Classic → Blind → Chaos         |
| Burning all Classic rerolls by round 6 | Dead boards later force bad locks        | Keep one reroll for emergencies |
| Copying a Classic sniper into Chaos    | Hidden position punishes one-note sheets | Draft multi-job attributes      |
| Ignoring mode-gated achievements       | Half the board stays locked              | Mix Blind/Chaos title hunts     |
