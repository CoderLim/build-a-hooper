## Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how `buildahooper.org` handles information when you use the Build a Hooper simulator, scoring calculator, guides, account features, achievements, and leaderboards.

## Who Operates This Site

`buildahooper.org` is independently operated by the Build a Hooper community maintainers. It is not an official NBA product. For privacy, ownership, or data requests, contact [support@buildahooper.org](mailto:support@buildahooper.org).

## What We Collect

We may collect limited technical and usage information such as browser type, device information, language preference, referrer data, page visits, and basic interaction events needed to operate and improve the site. If analytics or advertising tools are enabled, they are used for site performance, traffic understanding, advertising measurement, fraud prevention, and product improvement.

When you create an account or submit a completed run, we also store account identifiers (such as email), profile details you provide, and career statistics needed for achievements and leaderboards.

## Game Content

The playable simulator runs directly on `buildahooper.org`. Game progress may be stored locally in your browser. If you sign in and submit a completed run, the service stores the run and derived career statistics needed for profiles, achievements, and leaderboards.

## Cookies and Similar Technologies

We may use cookies or similar technologies to remember interface preferences, including language settings, and to support normal site operation.

Google advertising may load on selected content pages to support the site, measure ads, and help Google verify the site. Google Analytics may also load to measure traffic and site performance. These tools can load as part of normal page delivery; this site does **not** currently show a separate cookie consent banner.

Third-party vendors, including Google, may use cookies, web beacons, IP addresses, device identifiers, and similar technologies to serve ads, measure ad performance, prevent invalid activity, and understand prior visits to this site or other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables Google and its partners to serve ads based on your visit to this site and/or other sites on the Internet.

You can opt out of personalized advertising through [Google Ads Settings](https://www.google.com/settings/ads). You can also use browser settings to block or delete cookies. More detail on Google advertising technologies is available at [How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites) and [Advertising](https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads).

Visitors in the EEA, UK, and Switzerland can use Google Ads Settings and browser controls to limit personalized ads and cookies. We do not intentionally send personally identifiable information to Google in ad requests.

## How We Use Information

We use information to operate the website, deliver localized content, improve usability, monitor performance, prevent abuse and invalid traffic, show or measure advertising on content pages, run analytics, and understand how visitors discover and use the site.

## Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Information may be shared only with infrastructure, analytics, or advertising providers that help operate, measure, protect, or monetize the site, or when required to comply with law, protect rights, or address security issues.

## Data Retention

We keep information only for as long as reasonably necessary to operate the site, analyze performance, comply with legal obligations, and resolve abuse or security issues.

## Your Choices and Rights

You can stop using the site at any time, block cookies through your browser settings, play as a guest without cloud saves, or opt out of personalized Google ads through Ads Settings.

If you have an account, you may email [support@buildahooper.org](mailto:support@buildahooper.org) to request access to, correction of, or deletion of account-related personal data we hold, subject to legal retention needs (for example, abuse prevention). We aim to respond within a reasonable period.

## Third-Party Links and Services

This site may link to third-party services. Their privacy practices are governed by their own policies, not this one.

## Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us at [support@buildahooper.org](mailto:support@buildahooper.org).
